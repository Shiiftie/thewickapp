Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026
The short version: Wick collects your email address to send you product updates and your waitlist confirmation. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising. You can unsubscribe at any time.
01
Who We Are

Wick is a trading journal and community platform built for retail traders. Wick is operated by Seth Slokan, based in Baltimore, Maryland, USA. You can reach us at seth@thewickapp.com.

Our website is located at thewickapp.com.

02
What Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide to us. Currently this includes:

Email address — when you join our waitlist, we collect your email address to notify you when Wick launches and to send product updates.

When the full app launches, we may also collect your username, trading session data you log, and payment information processed securely through Stripe.

We do not collect any sensitive financial data, trading account credentials, or personal identification beyond what is listed above.

03
How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to:

Send you a welcome email confirming your spot on the Wick waitlist.

Notify you when Wick launches on August 1st, 2026.

Send occasional product updates about Wick features and progress.

We will never use your email for anything outside of Wick-related communications. We do not send spam. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.

04
How We Store Your Data

Your email address is stored securely with Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), our email marketing platform. Brevo is GDPR compliant and maintains industry-standard security practices.

We do not store your email address on our own servers or in any unencrypted format. You can read Brevo's privacy policy at brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy.

05
Do We Share Your Data?

No. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The only third party that has access to your email address is Brevo, which we use solely for the purpose of sending you emails from Wick.

06
Your Rights

You have the right to:

Unsubscribe at any time — every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you will be removed immediately.

Request deletion of your data — email us at seth@thewickapp.com and we will remove your information from our systems within 30 days.

Access your data — you can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you by emailing us.

07
Cookies

Our landing page uses localStorage (a browser storage mechanism) to remember the waitlist counter on your device. This is not a tracking cookie and does not collect any personal information.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third party analytics at this time.

08
Children's Privacy

Wick is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with their information please contact us at seth@thewickapp.com.

09
Changes to This Policy

As Wick grows we may update this privacy policy. When we do we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant we will notify waitlist members by email.

10
Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data please reach out:

Email: seth@thewickapp.com
